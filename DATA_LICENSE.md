# Data license and provenance

The data in this package (`data/`, `cache/`, `corpus/`, `metrics*/`,
`reports/`) is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International license (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Sources and attribution

- **Systematic mapping study (SMS) catalog.** `corpus/source.xlsx` and the
  labels of every excerpt come from the data extraction of the SMS by
  Furtado, OliveiraJr and Kalinowski, published under CC BY 4.0:
  Viviane R. Furtado, Edson OliveiraJr, and Marcos Kalinowski. 2020.
  *Replication Package: Systematic Mapping Study on Software Product Line
  Experiments.* Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3893302
  The sheet "Extracted Data" of `corpus/source.xlsx` has the same 211 studies
  as that record; in the 22 columns used as labels, 3,385 cells have the same
  text and 12 differ only in letter case, a trailing "---" marker or one
  encoded character.
- **Excerpts.** The texts in `data/**/instances.csv`,
  `data/**/grounded_instances.csv` and `cache/gemini_grounding.jsonl` are
  short verbatim excerpts of 200 published, peer-reviewed papers, located in
  their full texts. Copyright of these excerpts remains with the papers'
  authors and publishers; they are included as short quotations for research
  purposes, each linked to its source study (`study_id`, `corpus/catalog.csv`).
  The full-text PDFs are **not** distributed.
- **Synthetic paraphrases.** Rows with `origin = synthetic` and
  `cache/gemini_paraphrases.jsonl` were generated by Google Gemini
  (gemini-2.5-flash) from the excerpts above.

## Ethics

The data contains no personal data and no human-subject data: only text of
published scientific papers, bibliographic metadata, labels and model
predictions.
